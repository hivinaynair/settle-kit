# M3.T4: Integrate Solana attention, recovery and repayment evidence

Status: planned; no implementation in this change.

**Outcome:** Solana exceptions retain original operations, and recovery cases close only with
labeled authorized repayment evidence.

**Learn:** Operational recovery, finalized refund verification, rent protection and human
accountability.

- Depends on: [M3.T1](M3.T1.md), [M2.T11](M2.T11.md), [M2.T7](M2.T7.md).

- Policy: D17, D54, D114, D129, D232–D234; X6, X20. Invariants: I1, I4, I8, I10, I11.

## Checkpoint 1: Expose the original exception and permitted operator action

- [ ] Reviewed with Vinay; focused checks and remaining evidence recorded.

**Build:** Integrate J14.S9 and J15.S1 with saved signatures, blockhash lifetime, source message and
plain-language next action/reference. Transport failures remain retrieval errors. Runbooks identify
cluster, vault, asset, limits and fixed refund ATA/owner before external calls.

**Check:** Check unauthorized cases, unknown execution, expired accepted messages, read errors,
forwarding delays and quote shortage (J14.S9.c1–c4/J15.S1.c1–c2). None can instruct another payer
transfer as recovery.

**Walk through:** Which problem can the payer solve, and which requires the business to act?

## Checkpoint 2: Verify source recovery without creating settlement credit

- [ ] Reviewed with Vinay; focused checks and remaining evidence recorded.

**Build:** Observe operator/third-party J15.S2 instructions through finalized scans. Verify actual
asset movement and saved refund destination; attribute over-limit USDC as a drain, preserve
rent/payment receipts and keep unsupported mechanics unverified.

**Check:** Exercise J15.S3.c1–c3 with repeated recovery, missing inner evidence, non-USDC assets and
failed transactions. The source refund receipt never becomes Base backing, and an asset rescue does
not advance USDC memberships.

**Walk through:** Why is money reaching the host refund account still not proof that the payer was
repaid?

## Checkpoint 3: Close only an evidenced repayment case

- [ ] Reviewed with Vinay; focused checks and remaining evidence recorded.

**Build:** Reuse M3.T1’s authenticated D129 resolution transaction with finalized Solana or safe EVM
chain evidence and explicit host-reported fallback. Keep beneficiary/full-resolution confirmation
and immutable recovery/resolution history.

**Check:** Check J15.S4–S6 for missing/partial/disputed repayment, original-refund-only evidence,
conflicting reuse, unauthorized closure and crashes. Preserve credits and open a linked
investigation on contradictory later evidence.

**Walk through:** What does chain-verified establish, and which beneficiary claim remains the host’s
responsibility?

**Out of scope:** SDK-submitted recoveries, automatic payer refunds, Token-2022 rescue, rent
sweeping and a new dashboard/resolve API.
