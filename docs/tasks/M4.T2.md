## Checkpoint 1: Prepare a compact demonstration

- [ ] Reviewed with Vinay; focused checks and remaining evidence recorded.

**Build:** Script wallet consent, manual instructions, source-to-Base matching, committed host
credit and one duplicate/restart case. Show readable alerts and the experimental contract limits.

**Check:** Walk J11.S2.c1–c2 against actual recorded receipts and recovery evidence. Prepare
locally; no public publishing or outreach is implied.

**Walk through:** What is the one difficult failure case that best demonstrates how the system
protects money?

## Checkpoint 2: Make the repo easy to evaluate

- [ ] Reviewed with Vinay; focused checks and remaining evidence recorded.

**Build:** Link runnable setup, actual code paths, explorer receipts and threat-model notes from the
README. Write a short architecture explanation covering the host custody boundary and limits of the
guarantees.

**Check:** Audit each claim against J11.S2.c2–c4. Distinguish simulation, forks, testnet and
mainnet; remove unsupported production or exactly-once claims.

**Walk through:** Explain what Settle Kit owns and what remains the business's credit liability.

## Checkpoint 3: Rehearse the engineering explanation

- [ ] Reviewed with Vinay; focused checks and remaining evidence recorded.

**Build:** Draft a concise public post and record a short video. Rehearse payer consent, safe
evidence, durable retries, allocation and host deduplication from actual files. Vinay chooses when
and where to publish.

**Check:** A reader can reach setup, payment evidence and recovery explanation from the README
(J11.S2.c3). Do not add synthetic tests for prose or contact founders automatically.

**Walk through:** Explain one tradeoff, its rejected alternative and the evidence that supports the
chosen approach.

**Out of scope:** Docs website, formal npm release, dashboards and automatically contacting
founders.
